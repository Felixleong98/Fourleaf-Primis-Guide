# ⚔️ Blood Temple & Finale

### To the outpost
**Given:** Shogun Navita — `596, 72, 772`  
**Prerequisite:** There and back again  
**Turn in:** Pyrgopolynices Synnodus — `687, 24, 977`

**Objectives:** Speak to the Outpost Officer; kill The Robed Shadow; kill The Observer.

**Rewards:** 190 currency; Blood Hood, Blood Vest, Blood Leggings and Blood Greaves each have a separate 25% chance; Elite Scraps (various tiers).

### Blood and Fire
**Given:** Pyrgopolynices Synnodus  
**Prerequisite:** To the outpost

Continues the assault on the Blood Temple.

### The Four Guardians
**Given:** Pyrgopolynices Synnodus  
**Prerequisite:** Blood and Fire

The four guardians block the route toward the temple core.

### Legacy of the Flame
**Given:** Theophylactus Junianus — `1128, 90, 1045`  
**Prerequisite:** The Four Guardians  
**Turn in:** Theophylactus Junianus

**Objective:** Kill 1 Fire Elemental.

**Sanctum:** Speak with Granus Teletiamus at `1193, 151, 1002`.

**Rewards:** 300 currency; Elite Scraps (various tiers).

### There and Back Again
**Given:** Theophylactus Junianus — `1128, 90, 1045`  
**Prerequisite:** Legacy of the Flame  
**Turn in:** Shogun Navita — `596, 72, 772`

**Objective:** Return to Pontis Mille and speak to Navita in the Throne Room.

**Rewards:** 333 currency; Elite Scraps (various tiers).

### Goodbye Primis!
**Given:** Shogun Navita — `596, 72, 772`  
**Prerequisite:** There and Back Again  
**Turn in:** Captain Squalus — `1399, 19, 372`

**Objective:** Return to the beached ship and speak to Captain Squalus.

**Rewards:** 366 currency; Eternal Blood shield; Elite Scraps (various tiers).
