# ⚓ Caeruleaum

## Main quests

### Shipwrecked!
**Given:** Captain Squalus — `1399, 19, 372`  
**Prerequisite:** None  
**Turn in:** First Mate Iracundi — `1237, 37, 311`

**Objective:** Speak to the village leader at the lighthouse.

**Rewards:** 20 currency; 20 Bread; Shipwreck's Sword, Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings and Boots.

### Strangers in need...
**Given:** First Mate Iracundi — `1237, 37, 311`  
**Prerequisite:** Shipwrecked!  
**Turn in:** Chief Piscis — `1240, 38, 309`

**Objective:** Speak to the village leader in Caeruleaum.

**Reward:** 50 currency.

### Bandit Camp!
**Given:** Chief Piscis — `1240, 38, 309`  
**Prerequisite:** Strangers in need...

**Objectives**
- Kill 5 Acolyte Brutes
- Kill 4 Acolyte Sharpshooters
- Kill 1 Ice Mage
- Recover the Lost Heirloom

**Rewards:** 90 currency; Enchanted Book (Smite).

### Blue flower path!
**Given:** Chief Piscis  
**Prerequisite:** Bandit Camp!

Continues the route toward the Crystal Elemental.
