# 🌾 Farmstead & Mountain Pass

### The Crystal Elemental
**Given:** Petrae — `1368, 56, 575`  
**Prerequisite:** Blue flower path!  
**Turn in:** Eartep — `1409, 34, 593`

**Objectives:** Kill 1 Quartz/Crystal Elemental; speak to Eartep at the mountain-pass exit.

**Rewards:** 70 currency; Enchanted Book (Unbreaking).

### The Farmstead
**Given:** Eartep — `1409, 34, 593`  
**Prerequisite:** The Crystal Elemental  
**Turn in:** Filius — `1283, 21, 708`

**Objective:** Speak to the farmstead proprietor.

**Reward:** 70 currency.

### The path to Gladius
**Given:** Filius — `1283, 21, 708`  
**Prerequisite:** The Farmstead  
**Turn in:** General Hasta — `830, 27, 662`

**Objectives:** Kill 9 Undead Swashbucklers; 3 Undead Buccaneers; 1 Undead Captain; speak to the Lord of Gladius.

**Rewards:** 80 currency; Enchanted Book (Protection).
