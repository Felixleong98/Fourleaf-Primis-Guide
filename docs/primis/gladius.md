# 🛡️ Gladius

## Main quests

### Recovering Hope
**Given:** General Hasta — `830, 27, 662`  
**Prerequisite:** The path to Gladius

**Objective:** Retrieve the Assistance Agreement / Promissory Note from the mansion treasury.

**Rewards:** 90 currency; Gladius sword; Elite Scraps (various tiers).

### Gladius' Champion
**Given:** General Hasta  
**Prerequisite:** Recovering Hope

**Objective:** Find and defeat Fallen Champion Natus near the Statue.

**Rewards:** 100 currency; Enchanted Book (Projectile Protection).

### Bridging the gap
**Given:** General Hasta  
**Prerequisite:** Gladius' Champion  
**Turn in:** Shogun Navita — `596, 72, 772`

**Objectives:** Kill the Gatekeeper automaton at the bridge; speak to Shogun Navita.

**Rewards:** 100 currency; Pontis Mille Helm; Elite Scraps (various tiers).

### Taking back the streets
**Objectives:** Kill 10 Invading Swordsmen; 3 Invading Captains; 5 Invading Longbowmen; 5 Undead Villagers.

**Rewards:** 90 currency; Enchanted Book (Bane of Arthropods).

### Supplying more problems
**Objectives:** Retrieve The Good Stuff from the tavern basement; optionally defeat the corrupted Barkeep.

**Rewards:** 100 currency; Elite Scraps (various tiers).

### A sinking feeling
**Objectives:** Kill 1 Invading Admiral; kill 10 Invading Mariners on the ships outside Gladius.

**Rewards:** 70 currency; Elite Scraps (various tiers).

### Sunken Ship
**Objectives:** Kill 1 Invading Mariner at the sunken ship; retrieve 3 Important Documents.

**Rewards:** 80 currency; 5 Cooked Cod.
