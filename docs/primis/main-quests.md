## 🎯 Objective summary

### Quests 1–10
- **Shipwrecked!** — reach the lighthouse village.
- **Strangers in need...** — speak with the village leader.
- **Bandit Camp!** — defeat the listed Acolytes and Ice Mage; recover the heirloom.
- **Blue flower path!** — follow the blue flowers and locate the mountain guide.
- **The Crystal Elemental** — defeat the Quartz/Crystal Elemental and meet Eartep.
- **The Farmstead** — speak with the farmstead proprietor.
- **The path to Gladius** — clear the Undead forces and speak with Gladius' lord.
- **Recovering Hope** — retrieve the Assistance Agreement/Promissory Note.
- **Gladius' Champion** — defeat Fallen Champion Natus.
- **Bridging the gap** — defeat the Gatekeeper automaton and speak with Navita.

### Quests 11–20
- **Sending a message!** — report to Fortes Bellator.
- **Mine path** — report to the Royal Guard Centurion.
- **Goblins in the mines!** — clear the listed goblin groups and Goblin King.
- **Storm's end** — defeat the Storm Elemental and temple forces.
- **Severance payback!** — defeat Counselor Machiavelli and recover the Stone of Far Speech.
- **The ice village awaits** — ask travelers for directions and reach Glacies.
- **Helping the north** — speak with three villagers.
- **The trial of ice** — defeat a Minor Ice Elemental and Fallen Trial Warrior. Required side quests: Catch Me If You Can, Taking the Bait, Get Them Off My Back!, and Make Them Go Ouch.
- **Chill out** — defeat the Ice Elemental in the temple.
- **Terravilla** — meet Overseer Lutum.

### Quests 21–30
- **Taking out the trash** — defeat Sluggers and Robber Gladiators.
- **Big trouble in a little pub** — defeat the Bar Brawlers.
- **Strike the earth** — navigate the ruins maze and defeat the Earth Elemental.
- **There and Back Again** — report Terravilla's support to Navita.
- **To the outpost** — report to the forward outpost.
- **Blood and Fire** — fight through to the Blood Temple entrance.
- **The Four Guardians** — defeat the four barrier guardians.
- **Legacy of the Flame** — defeat the Fire Elemental; Granus Teletiamus at 1193, 151, 1002 provides Sanctum access.
- **There and Back Again** — return to Pontis Mille's throne room.
- **Goodbye Primis!** — return to the repaired ship.
