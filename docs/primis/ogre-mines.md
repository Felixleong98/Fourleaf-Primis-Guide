# ⛏️ Ogre Mines

### Ogre-sized problems
**Given:** Metus  
**Prerequisite:** The Crystal Elemental  
**Turn in:** Vetus Fossores — `1470, 60, 557`

**Objectives:** Kill 2 Undead Swordsmen; kill 1 Undead Archer.

**Reward:** 80 currency.

### Lost Bretheren
**Given:** Fugit Fossores  
**Prerequisite:** The Crystal Elemental  
**Turn in:** Fugit Fossores

**Objective:** Find and speak to Fortis Fossores in the mines.

**Rewards:** 70 currency; Lucky Lickaxe; Elite Scraps (various tiers).

### Lucky Pickaxe
**Given:** Vetus Fossores — `1470, 60, 557`  
**Prerequisite:** The Crystal Elemental

**Objectives:** Find and kill the Ogre Magi; retrieve the Lucky Pickaxe.

**Rewards:** 90 currency; Elite Lucky Ticket.
