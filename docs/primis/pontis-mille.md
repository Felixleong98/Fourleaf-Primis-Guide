# 🏛️ Pontis Mille

## Main quests

### Sending a message!
**Given:** Shogun Navita — `596, 72, 772`  
**Prerequisite:** Bridging the gap  
**Turn in:** Fortes Bellator — `593, 77, 748`

**Objective:** Speak to Major Fortes Bellator in the Throne Room.

**Rewards:** 30 currency; Pontis Mille Boots.

### Mine path
**Given:** Fortes Bellator — `593, 77, 748`  
**Prerequisite:** Sending a message!  
**Turn in:** Royal Guard Centurion — `642, 50, 460`

**Objective:** Speak to the Royal Guard Centurion at the Mines Entrance.

**Rewards:** 40 currency; Pontis Mille Leggings.

### Goblins in the mines!
**Given:** Royal Guard Centurion — `642, 50, 460`  
**Prerequisite:** Mine path  
**Turn in:** Blepharo Octavius — `792, 102, 427`

**Objectives:** Kill 5 Goblin Warriors; 5 Goblin Grunts; 4 Goblin Spearmen; 1 Goblin King.

**Rewards:** 110 currency; Pontis Mille Chestplate.

### Storm's end
**Given:** Blepharo Octavius — `792, 102, 427`  
**Prerequisite:** Goblins in the mines!  
**Turn in:** Shogun Navita

**Objectives:** Kill 1 Storm Elemental; 5 Storm Temple Axemen; 6 Storm Temple Bowmen; 5 Minor Storm Elementals; 3 Storm Temple Summoners.

**Rewards:** 130 currency; Pontis Mille Katana.

### Severance payback!
**Given:** Shogun Navita — `596, 72, 772`  
**Prerequisite:** Storm's end

**Objectives:** Kill Counselor Machiavelli; retrieve the Stone of Far Speech.

**Rewards:** 140 currency; Pontis Mille Shield; Elite Scraps (various tiers).

### The ice village awaits
**Given:** Shogun Navita — `596, 72, 772`  
**Prerequisite:** Severance payback!  
**Turn in:** Glacies Princeps — `925, 46, 1483`

**Objectives:** Ask three travelers for directions; speak to Glacies at the Ice Village.

**Rewards:** 140 currency; Enchanted Book (Fire Protection).
