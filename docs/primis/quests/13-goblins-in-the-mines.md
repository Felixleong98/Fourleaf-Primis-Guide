# Goblins in the mines!

**Type:** Main  
**Region:** Pontis Mille  
**Given by:** Royal Guard Centurion  
**Prerequisite:** Mine path  
**Turn in:** Blepharo Octavius — `792, 102, 427`

## 🎯 Objectives
- Kill 5 Goblin Warriors
- Kill 5 Goblin Grunts
- Kill 4 Goblin Spearmen
- Kill 1 Goblin King

## 🎁 Rewards
- 110 currency
- Pontis Mille Chestplate
