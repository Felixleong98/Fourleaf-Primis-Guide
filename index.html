About this wiki

This site reorganizes factual Primis quest information into a player-friendly Fourleaf SMP reference. Quest names, NPCs, prerequisites, objectives and reward information are paraphrased rather than copied as source prose.

Reward rolls may be independent or chance-based where noted. Coordinates shown in the original guide are rounded NPC positions; use in-game quest tracking for exact destinations.
